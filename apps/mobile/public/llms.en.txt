# Durak Master

> Online Durak: throw-in and transfer variants, tables for 2–6 players, against people or bots. Browser, Android and iOS from one codebase.

Durak Master is a free card game. The rules are the classic ones: throw-in and
transfer Durak, a deck of 24, 36 or 52 cards, with or without jokers. A table seats
two to six players, and empty seats can be filled with bots. In-game credits are
earned by playing — they cannot be bought, sold or transferred between players, and
there is no wagering of real money.

## Pages

- [Home](https://durakmaster.ru/): sign in, the list of open tables, creating your own.

The app is a single page: the lobby, the table and the profile live at one address and
switch on the client.

## Game

- Modes: throw-in, transfer
- Deck: 24, 36 or 52 cards, jokers optional
- Players: 2–6 at a table, empty seats take bots
- Speed: normal (30 seconds a turn) or fast (15 seconds)
- Interface languages: Russian, English

## Technology

- Client: Expo SDK 57, React Native 0.86, Expo Router — one codebase for browser, Android and iOS
- Server: NestJS on Bun, Prisma 7, PostgreSQL
- Realtime: WebSocket, table state held in server memory
- Game rules: a separate package of pure functions, with no transport and no database
- Auth: better-auth, sign-in by email
