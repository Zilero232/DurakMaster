# Privacy Policy

**DurakMaster** — last updated 30 August 2026.

This is the source of the policy that must be published at a public URL before
the Google Play listing can go live. Keep it in step with
[privacy.md](./privacy.md), which holds the matching Data safety answers — a
policy that promises less than the app collects is grounds for rejection.

Replace `support@durakmaster.ru` with the address you actually read.

---

## What we collect

We collect only what the game needs to work.

| Data | Why we need it | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Your account: signing in, recovering access | Yes |
| Display name | Shown to other players at the table | No, a default is generated |
| Avatar — a preset or an image you upload | Shown to other players | No |
| Game history, rating, in-game credits and coins | Ranking and your own profile | Yes |

We do **not** collect your location, your contacts, your device identifiers, or
any advertising data. There is no analytics SDK and no ad network in the app.

## How we use it

Your data is used to run the game: to sign you in, seat you at a table, show you
to other players, and keep your rating and balance. Nothing else.

## Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.

Other players at your table see only your display name, avatar, rating and the
public parts of your game history. They never see your email address.

## In-game currency

Credits and coins are earned by playing. They cannot be bought, sold, or
transferred between players, and they have no value outside the game.

## How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you ask us to delete it, we
remove your account and the personal data attached to it. Aggregate game
statistics that no longer identify you may be retained.

## Deleting your account

Write to **support@durakmaster.ru** from the address your account uses. We
delete the account and its personal data within 30 days.

## Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS and WSS.
Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we
will remove it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes
will be announced in the app.
